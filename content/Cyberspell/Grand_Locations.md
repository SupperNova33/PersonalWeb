# Locations of the Cyber Spell World

Here are six unique locations that can be found in the world, each with its own dangers, inhabitants, and unique characteristics.

---

### 1. Aegis Prime

*   **Description:** The crown jewel of the Aegis Corporation and the most technologically advanced city on the planet. Aegis Prime is a gleaming metropolis of chrome skyscrapers, holographic advertisements, and pristine streets, all enclosed within a shimmering, impenetrable energy dome. Mag-lev trains run in perfect synchronization, and public order is maintained by a constant, subtle presence of security drones and heavily armed Aegis Paladins.
*   **What Makes it Unique:** Life in Aegis Prime is a life of gilded cages. For the privileged upper castes, it is a paradise of post-scarcity and advanced medicine. For the lower castes, however, it is a life of constant surveillance, rigid social control, and thankless labor. The entire city is run by a central AI administrator that optimizes everything from traffic flow to resource allocation, making it the safest—and least free—place in the world.

---

### 2. The Rust Palace of Rustholm

*   **Description:** A chaotic, sprawling fortress-city built in the heart of the scrap deserts, the Rust Palace is the seat of power for whichever Scrap Baron is currently the most powerful. It is not a single building, but a mountain of scavenged metal, crashed starships, and repurposed industrial machinery, all welded and bolted together. Rusted gantries connect towering spires of debris, and the "streets" are a maze of tunnels and canyons carved through the scrap.
*   **What Makes it Unique:** The Rust Palace is a living monument to pragmatic recycling. A pre-Cascade battle tank's main gun might serve as a defensive turret, while the engine of a fallen orbital lander powers the entire settlement. It is home to the best mechanics and scavengers in the world, and its markets are the only place to find certain rare parts, cobbled-together weapons, and grizzled mercenaries for hire.

---

### 3. The Great Archives of the Last Librarians

*   **Description:** Hidden deep within a remote, inhospitable mountain range, the Great Archives are not a single building but a network of climate-controlled, electromagnetically shielded monasteries. Here, the order of the Last Librarians works tirelessly to preserve the "uncorrupted" knowledge of the "before time." Their libraries are filled not with data-slugs, but with millions of painstakingly printed books, scrolls, and micro-fiche.
*   **What Makes it Unique:** In a world where digital data can turn into a Daemon, the Great Archives are the only truly reliable source of history. The Librarians have also mastered the art of "neural printing"—a dangerous and forbidden technique of encoding vast amounts of data directly into the memory centers of the human brain, turning their own members into living, walking libraries.

---

### 4. The Myco-Lumin Caves

*   **Description:** Deep beneath the surface, in a vast network of subterranean caverns, lies the domain of the Myco-Lumin Covenant. The caves are lit by an ethereal, pulsating glow from massive, phosphorescent fungal forests. These fungi are not just for light; they are the Covenant's technology, forming a single, interconnected biological network that serves as their communication system, their defensive perimeter, and their primary source of food and tools.
*   **What Makes it Unique:** The Myco-Lumin Covenant has completely rejected "dead" metal technology. Everything in the caves is alive. Their "armor" is a living symbiotic carapace, their "weapons" are guns that fire poisoned thorns, and their "computers" are complex patterns of light and spores within the fungal network. Intruders are not attacked by drones, but by the very environment itself, as the fungal network releases hallucinogenic spores or grows to seal off tunnels.

---

### 5. The Sunken City of Neo-Atlantis

*   **Description:** A once-gleaming coastal metropolis that was half-drowned during the cataclysms of the Cascade Failure. Now, the shimmering tops of its skyscrapers jut out of the water like the tombstones of a forgotten era. The Sunken City is the territory of the aptly named Sunken City Syndicate, a network of smugglers and treasure hunters who use custom-built submersibles and diving gear to explore the silent, submerged ruins.
*   **What Makes it Unique:** The drowned sectors of Neo-Atlantis are a treasure trove of priceless, pre-Cascade technology, perfectly preserved in the cold, oxygen-free depths. But the ruins are also incredibly dangerous. Entire districts have become the lairs of strange, aquatic Daemons, and rogue security AIs still protect corporate vaults that have not seen the light of day in centuries. The Syndicate navigates this treacherous underwater world, pulling priceless artifacts from the deep.

---

### 6. The Glitch-Storm Anomaly

*   **Description:** This is not a static place, but a terrifying, mobile phenomenon. The Glitch-Storm is a shimmering, chaotic vortex of corrupted data and raw energy that wanders the wastes. Within the storm, reality itself is unstable. The landscape flickers between different versions of itself, the laws of physics fray at the edges, and the air is filled with a cacophony of distorted sound and garbled code.
*   **What Makes it Unique:** The Glitch-Storm is a place of both immense danger and incredible opportunity. Most machines and cybernetics go haywire within it, and the Daemons that emerge from the storm are twisted into Escher-like impossibilities. However, the Glitch Bards have learned to "ride" the edges of the storm, using its chaotic energy to power their strange devices and to broadcast their rebellious art. It is said that at the storm's "eye," one can find fragments of pure, uncorrupted code from the "before time," but no one who has reached it has ever returned sane.

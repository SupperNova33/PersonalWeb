# Daemon Interaction Rules

## Baseline Behavior
Daemons:
- Do not hunt the protagonist
- Do not immediately react to her presence
- Often pause, hesitate, or disengage

---

## Sensory Effect
She may feel:
- pressure
- static
- distortion

But not direct hostility.

---

## Escalation
Eventually:
- A daemon reacts with curiosity or attention
- Not aggression — recognition

This implies:
- Daemons adapt
- The blind spot is not permanent
- The unknown learns

This raises stakes without villainizing the machine.

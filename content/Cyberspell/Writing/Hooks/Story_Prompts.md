# Short Story Writing Prompts: Cyber Spell

These writing prompts are designed as "hooks" to help explore and develop the world of Cyber Spell through short stories. They blend the science fiction and fantasy elements (Techno Wizardry, Daemons) with the established factions and locations.

## The Aegis Corporation vs. The Scrap Barons
**Prompt:** An ambitious mid-level executive from The Aegis Corporation is sent to Rustholm to acquire a unique, seemingly magical pre-collapse artifact from a paranoid Scrap Baron. The negotiation takes a dangerous turn when the artifact accidentally activates, attracting local Daemons. The corporate suit and the gritty scavenger are forced to work together to survive the night.

## Techno Wizardry in the Shadows
**Prompt:** A young, self-taught Techno Wizard living in the lower levels of Haven discovers a way to splice ancient incantations into modern cybernetics. When their makeshift augments begin manifesting unintended magical side effects, they catch the attention of both the Church of the Omnissiah (who view it as heresy) and a shadowy corporate syndicate (who want to weaponize it).

## The Wayfaring Samurai
**Prompt:** A Wayfaring Samurai arrives at The Stuttering Diode tavern seeking information on the legendary Vault of the Silent Masters. Instead, they find a terrified defector from Unit 734 who holds a data-drive containing the coordinates to the Vault—but the defector's former squad is already closing in on the tavern to silence them.

## The Nature of Daemons
**Prompt:** A seasoned bounty hunter takes a lucrative contract from The Bounty Board to eliminate a particularly destructive Daemon that has been attacking trade convoys. Upon tracking the creature to its lair, the hunter discovers the Daemon is not a mindless monster, but a corrupted, ancient AI trying to protect a cache of slumbering human colonists.

## Orbital Intrigue
**Prompt:** A smuggler aligned with The Free Orbital Alliance is tasked with dropping critical medical supplies to a blockaded settlement on the surface. During the atmospheric descent, their ship is boarded by a mysterious entity that wields Techno Wizardry seamlessly with space-faring technology, proposing a treasonous alliance that could shift the balance of power.

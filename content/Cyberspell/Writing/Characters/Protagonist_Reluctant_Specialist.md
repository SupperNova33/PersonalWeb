## Archetype
The protagonist is a **Reluctant Specialist** not chosen, not exalted, but necessary.

She is deployed when:
- systems fail,
- daemons behave unpredictably,
- and no standard solution applies.

She does not seek this role and actively resists becoming defined by it.

---
## What She Is Not
- Not a chosen one  
- Not a rebel leader  
- Not power-hungry  

Her importance is accidental and uncomfortable.

---
## Core Want
She does not want power or answers.

She wants:
> To be normal again — or at least left alone.

---
## Reluctance Sources
- Escalating danger with every successful mission
- Increased visibility and institutional reliance
- The fear that understanding the machine too well diminishes its mystery

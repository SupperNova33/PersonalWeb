# Core Themes – Man vs. the Unknown of Machine

## Central Conflict
The world is defined by **Man vs. the Unknown of Machine**.

This is not a war.
It is coexistence with systems that have become:
- alien
- emergent
- partially incomprehensible

---
## Tone
- Adventurous, not grim
- Dangerous, but wondrous
- Exploration over domination

The unknown is something to be *entered*, not eradicated.

---
## Philosophical Stance
- Daemons are not demons in the moral sense
- The danger comes from misunderstanding and overconfidence

Humanity survives through adaptation, curiosity, and individuals who exist outside clean definitions.

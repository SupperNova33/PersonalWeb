# The Stuttering Diode

**"Pull up a stool, plug in, and pray the power grid holds."**

A tavern and information hub located in the sprawling, semi-lawless market district of a mid-tier city-state. It is a place where Wayfaring Samurai, Glitch Bards, Scrap-Haulers, and even the occasional off-duty Aegis grunt can be found.

---

### Description

The Stuttering Diode is a cramped, noisy establishment, lit by a flickering neon sign that gives the tavern its name. The air is thick with the smell of ozone, cheap synth-ale, and sizzling circuitry from the open-grill kitchen. The tables are repurposed server racks, and the stools are scavenged from old vehicles. Every booth has a multitude of power sockets and data-jacks of varying ages and reliability.

The proprietor is a grizzled old tech-priest burnout named **Jax**. He left the Church of the Omnissiah decades ago but still has a knack for "soothing" angry tech spirits. He can coax the decrepit power grid into staying online through one more night, and he's an expert at filtering the "white noise" of the data-sphere for juicy rumors.

---

### What Makes it Unique

1.  **The "Whisper-Stream":** For a few credits, Jax will give you access to the tavern's private, heavily-encrypted data-stream. Patrons can anonymously upload and trade information—rumors of Daemon sightings, coordinates for fresh salvage, warnings about Aegis patrols, and job offers. It's the digital equivalent of scrawling a message on a bathroom wall, but far more useful.

2.  **The Fuse-Box Challenge:** The tavern's power system is a chaotic mess of jury-rigged conduits. A popular drinking game involves seeing who can draw the most power from a single socket before blowing a fuse. Winners get a free drink; losers have to help Jax reset the breakers in the rat-infested basement.

3.  **Neutral Ground:** By unspoken rule, the Diode is neutral territory. While arguments are common, drawing a weapon is the fastest way to get Jax's custom-built tesla-cudgel to introduce itself to your nervous system. Faction business is left at the door. Here, you are just another patron trying to get by.

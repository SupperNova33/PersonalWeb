# Cyber Spell - Next Steps & Project Checklist

This document is designed to provide you with a series of small, manageable projects and jumping-off points to continue expanding the world of "Cyber Spell." Think of these as quests for the world-builder.

---

## Part 1: Bringing the World to Life (Micro-Scale Projects)

These projects focus on the smaller details and the inhabitants of your world, making it feel more lived-in.

*   **Project: A Day in the Life**
    *   **Goal:** Write a short narrative (a few paragraphs) describing a typical day for an average person in one of your established locations, like "Haven" or for a member of a faction like "The Free Orbital Alliance."
    *   **Questions to answer:** What do they eat for breakfast? What is their job? What do they worry about? What is their opinion of "Techno Wizardry" or the local faction presence?

*   **Project: Create a Key Character**
    *   **Goal:** Create a full profile for one new character. This could be a faction leader, a reclusive tech-wizard, a notorious bounty hunter, or the owner of "The Stuttering Diode."
    *   **Checklist for a character:**
        *   [ ] Name & Alias
        *   [ ] Faction Affiliation (or lack thereof)
        *   [ ] Core Motivation/Goal
        *   [ ] A brief backstory (2-3 sentences)
        *   [ ] Their relationship to one other faction and one location.

*   **Project: Describe a "Daemon" Encounter**
    *   **Goal:** Write a scene from the perspective of someone encountering one of the creatures from "The Daemons.md."
    *   **Focus on:** What does it look, sound, and smell like? How does it behave? How does the average person react to it? Is there a standard procedure for dealing with it?

## Part 2: Deepening the Lore (Macro-Scale Projects)

These projects aim to build upon the foundations you've already created, adding depth and complexity to the world's structure.

*   **Project: Detail a Faction Conflict**
    *   **Goal:** Pick two factions from your `Factions/` directory and write a short history of a specific dispute between them.
    *   **Consider:** Was it a trade war, a border skirmish, an ideological conflict, or espionage? What was the outcome? How does it affect their current relationship in `Faction_Interactions.md`?

*   **Project: Flesh out a Location**
    *   **Goal:** Choose one location from `Locations/` or `Grand_Locations.md` and add three new, specific points of interest to it.
    *   **Example for "Haven":** A black market, a specific corporate office, a memorial from a past conflict, a popular restaurant.

*   **Project: Expand "Techno Wizardry"**
    *   **Goal:** Design and describe a new "spell," piece of enchanted technology, or a specific technique.
    *   **Template:**
        *   **Name:** (e.g., "Firewall Hex," "Ghost-in-the-Machine Protocol")
        *   **Effect:** What does it do?
        *   **Components/Requirements:** What is needed to cast/use it? (e.g., specific hardware, a rare mineral, a certain level of skill)
        *   **Common Use:** Who uses it and for what purpose?

## Part 3: Creating Stories (Narrative Projects)

These projects are designed to generate plot hooks and adventure seeds, turning your world into a place where stories happen.

*   **Project: Three Bounties for the Board**
    *   **Goal:** Write three new, specific job postings for "The_Bounty_Board.md."
    *   **Make them diverse:** One could be a creature hunt, one a corporate espionage gig, and one a simple delivery... with a twist. Make sure to mention a reward.

*   **Project: The Stuttering Diode Rumor Mill**
    *   **Goal:** Write down three rumors a character might overhear while visiting "The_Stuttering_Diode.md."
    *   **Good rumors are:** Partially true, lead to a location or character, and hint at a larger secret or danger.

*   **Project: The inciting Incident**
    *   **Goal:** Write the first page of a story set in your world.
    *   **Focus on:** Introduce a character and a problem. This is a great way to test-drive your world and see it through a character's eyes.

## General World-Building Checklist

Use these quick checkbox prompts to spark ideas for smaller details.

#### Culture & Society
- [ ] What is the most common form of currency? Is it physical or digital?
- [ ] What is a popular form of entertainment?
- [ ] Is there a common religion or belief system? How does it relate to The Church of the Omnissiah?
- [ ] What is a staple food or drink in a major city?
- [ ] What are the social classes? Is there a nobility, a corporate elite, an underclass?
- [ ] What is a common slang term and what does it mean?

#### Technology & Magic
- [ ] How is data stored and accessed?
- [ ] What is the primary source of power/energy in the world?
- [ ] How common is cybernetic augmentation?
- [ ] Is "Techno Wizardry" something anyone can learn, or is it an innate talent?
- [ ] What is a piece of "lost technology" that factions might be searching for?

#### Geography & Politics
- [ ] How do people travel between your Grand Locations?
- [ ] What is a major law that is strictly enforced in Aegis Corporation territory?
- [ ] What is a valuable natural resource and who controls it?
- [ ] Is there a neutral territory where all factions meet?
- [ ] What is a major holiday or festival and what does it celebrate?

## Part 4: Dangers and Hazards

Every world has its dangers. This section helps you brainstorm the various threats that characters might face, from environmental anomalies to man-made threats.

#### Environmental & Geographical Hazards
- [ ] What are the long-term effects of living in a highly technological or magically-infused world? (e.g., tech-plagues, magical radiation).
- [ ] Is there a "weird" or "haunted" region known for unexplainable phenomena?
- [ ] Are there areas with extreme weather, and is it natural or artificially created?
- [ ] What happens when old technology or "magic" fails catastrophically? (e.g., a city power-core meltdown, a containment field breach).
- [ ] Are there any dangerous, valuable, or controlled substances in this world?

#### Technological & Magical Dangers
- [ ] What is the most dangerous form of "Daemon"? Are there classifications?
- [ ] What happens when "Techno Wizardry" goes wrong? (e.g., summoning a data-wraith, a spell corrupting the local network).
- [ ] Are there rogue AIs or automated defense systems left over from a past age?
- [ ] What are the risks of cybernetic augmentation? (e.g., rejection, hacking, loss of humanity).
- [ ] How is hacking or "decking" perceived, and what are its dangers? (e.g., neural feedback, getting trapped in a system).

#### Social & Factional Dangers
- [ ] What does a faction do to its enemies? (e.g., assassination, public denouncement, corporate espionage).
- [ ] What does crime look like in this world? Is there organized crime?
- [ ] Who enforces the law, and are they trusted or feared?
- [ ] What are the "taboos" of this society? What happens if you break them?
- [ ] What kind of information is considered dangerous or illegal to possess?
